Privacy

Chronocept Revolutions is a small, independent channel and website. Here's a plain-language summary of what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • If you subscribe for updates: your name, email address, country, and age group.
  • If you submit a CR: your name, email, country, your written submission, and a photo, unless you choose to submit anonymously, in which case no photo is required or stored.
  • If you use the site's contact button, it opens your own email app, we don't collect anything from that unless you choose to send us a message.

Why we collect it

To send you updates about new content if you've subscribed, to review and credit CR submissions, and to understand where our audience is located so we can cover more relevant countries.

Where it's stored

Subscriber and submission data is stored in Supabase, a hosted database provider. Uploaded photos and submissions are kept in private storage that isn't publicly browsable.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data to third parties. We don't use it for anything beyond running Chronocept Revolutions and considering your submission or subscription.

Removing your data

Email chronoceptrevolutions@gmail.com with the request, and we'll remove your information.
