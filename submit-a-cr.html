What to expect

What's expected of a submission

Your draft should follow the Issue / How Others Do It / Solution structure, be about a real, verifiable situation, and stay non-political in tone — evaluate what a country is doing, not who's in charge. Cite your sources; we will check them.

How it's reviewed

Every submission is checked against its sources, then edited by the CR team for structure, clarity, and length. You'll receive the edited version back for your own reference before anything is produced.
