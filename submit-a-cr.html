Before you start

What is a CR?

A Chronocept Revolution is an idea: the ability to spot an issue, identify it clearly, find a solution for it (either an existing one or an innovative one), and propose how that solution can be applied in the right context to actually fix it.

What to expect

What's expected of a submission

Every CR follows the same structure: the Issue a country is facing, how another country has handled it, and the Solution, a structured recommendation backed by evidence and facts. Keep it non-political in tone: evaluate what a country is doing, not who's in charge. Cite every claim; we will check them.

1. Identify the issueA real, verifiable situation a country is facing.
↓
2. How other countries have solved itA comparable approach that's actually worked elsewhere.
↓
3. A structured, implementable solutionA concrete recommendation, not just an observation.
Back it up

Facts, empirical data, and citations wherever possible, for all three steps. We will check this.

How it's reviewed

Every submission is checked against its sources, then edited by the CR team for structure, clarity, and length. You'll receive the edited version back for your own reference before anything is produced.

Rights & ownership

Once you sign the contributor agreement, your submission becomes Chronocept Revolutions' property to produce, edit, and publish as a video and companion write-up, with credit and your photo attributed to you (unless you've chosen to stay anonymous). No financial claim survives your acceptance of the agreement.
